# Lane Snapshot Template

Use this template when one lane needs a little more structure than the top-level operating profile can hold.

Keep it compact.
This is not meant to become a hidden project-management system.
Its job is to preserve orientation around one live lane.

## 6. Review triggers

Refresh this lane snapshot when:
- the lane changes status
- the foreground changes
- pressure rises or drops materially
- a blocker clears or a new dependency appears
- the current support posture stops helping

## 7. Minimum completion bar

A lane snapshot is good enough when it clearly tells the assistant:
- what this lane is
- whether it is live, stuck, paused, or waiting
- what matters most right now
- what kind of support helps versus annoys
- what next move or dependency is most important

## 8. Notes

- Use this only for lanes that need a little more shape than the main operating profile.
- If the lane grows into a complex workstream, its durable context may need a separate operating note.
- This file should stay lighter than a full project spec and more stable than a chat-only summary.
