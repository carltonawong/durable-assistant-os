# Cadence Review

> Starter-pack later-use working copy.
> Do not fill this during first install unless there is already a real operating baseline.
> For the reusable source version, see `templates/cadence-review-template.md`.

## 1. Heartbeat / quick check-in

- What is foreground right now?
- What feels most at risk of slipping?
- Is anything newly urgent, blocked, or noisy?
- What is the smallest useful next move today?

## 2. Weekly review

- What moved this week?
- What stalled this week?
- Which lane carried the most pressure?
- Which reminders were useful versus noisy?
- What should be promoted, paused, or dropped?

## 3. Monthly reset

- Which lanes are still real and active?
- What should change in the current lane map?
- What has become clutter?
- What deserves a stronger support system?
- What should be simplified?
